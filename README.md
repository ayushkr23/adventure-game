# Whispering Woods Adventure

A simple browser-based text adventure game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Make choices to navigate through the story
3. Collect gold, potions, and items
4. Fight enemies by making strategic decisions
5. Reach the final shrine and defeat the guardian to win

## Features

- **Multiple Locations**: Village, Forest, Cave, and Shrine
- **Combat System**: Fight wolves, slimes, and the final guardian
- **Inventory System**: Collect items like potions, lanterns, and keys
- **Stats Tracking**: Monitor your HP, Gold, Power, and Items
- **Dynamic Story**: Different outcomes based on your choices
- **Save System**: Your progress is tracked during gameplay

## Game Stats

- **HP**: Your health points. Reach 0 and you lose
- **Gold**: Currency to buy potions and items
- **Power**: Increases your damage in combat
- **Items**: Collect useful items during your adventure

## Story Overview

You arrive at the Whispering Woods and must explore to find a shrine key, gather resources, and defeat the shrine guardian to save the village and complete your quest.

## No Installation Required

Just download `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser. No server or dependencies needed!
